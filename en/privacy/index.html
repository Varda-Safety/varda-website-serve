Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Varda (“Varda”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information on the website varda-safety.com and in the Varda application at app.varda-safety.com (together, the “Service”). Varda helps organizations receive, handle and prevent sexual harassment complaints.

Who is responsible for the information

Varda is provided to organizations (employers). Information about complaints, investigations and the people involved in them is entered by the organization and its users, and the organization decides what is collected and how it is used; we process it on the organization’s behalf and according to its instructions. For account and sign-in information, and for information sent to us through the website, we are responsible.

Information we collect

Account and sign-in information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. When you sign in with an email link, we receive your email address. We also store your role and the organization you belong to.

Information you enter into the Service. Reports, answers to questions, interview notes, documents and files you upload, and messages you exchange with the Service’s assistant. This may include sensitive information about you or about other people.

Technical information. A session cookie that keeps you signed in, and logs of activity in the application (such as sign-ins and changes to cases) that are kept for security and audit purposes.

Information sent through the website. If you fill in the contact form, we receive the details you enter (such as name, role, organization and contact details), and use them only to contact you.

Google user data

Varda requests only the basic Google sign-in permissions (openid, email and profile). We use the name, email address and profile picture we receive solely to identify you, create and maintain your account and display your name in the application. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the Service, comply with law, or protect security.

Varda’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use information

  • To provide the Service: sign you in, manage cases and guide the organization through the process.
  • To send messages related to the Service, such as sign-in links, invitations and case notifications.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse, and keep an audit trail of actions.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Artificial intelligence

Parts of the Service use AI models to analyze text and assist the user. The relevant text is sent to the AI provider only to produce a response, under terms that do not allow the provider to use it to train its models.

Service providers

We use providers that process information on our behalf, only as needed to operate the Service: cloud hosting and file storage (Vercel), a database (Neon), email delivery (Resend), website form delivery (FormSubmit), AI models (such as Anthropic), and Google for sign-in. Information may be stored and processed outside Israel, including in the United States, by providers that are bound to protect it.

We may also disclose information when required by law or by a competent authority.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access within the application is limited by role and organization, sign-in sessions are stored only as a cryptographic hash, and actions are recorded in an audit log. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect information to an appropriate standard.

Retention

We keep information for as long as the organization’s account is active, or as long as required for the purpose it was collected for or by law. When an organization ends its use of the Service, its information is deleted or returned according to its agreement with us.

Your rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, you may request to access the information about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. If your information was entered by your organization, you may contact your organization directly, or contact us and we will pass the request on. You may also revoke Varda’s access to your Google account at any time in your Google account settings.

Cookies

The application uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in. The website saves your choice of light or dark mode in your browser. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

The Service is intended for organizations and their adult employees, and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date of the latest update appears at the top of the page, and we will notify users of the application of material changes.

Contact

For questions or requests about privacy: contact@varda-safety.com.
